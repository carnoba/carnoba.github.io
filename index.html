Research, Kaggle & Market Analysis

Publication Zenodo · v1.0

Village-Disjoint Validation and Parsimonious Feature Selection for Climate-Sensitive Child Mortality Risk Prediction in Uganda

Author: Syed Muhammad Haris, Department of Artificial Intelligence, Dawood University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Karachi

Open-access preprint directly connected to the Zindi AI4EAC Climate Risk & Health Prediction Challenge. Presents 15-fold village-disjoint validation (>97% unseen test villages), a Kolmogorov–Smirnov (KS) covariate drift audit across geographic partitions, and a regularized three-model gradient boosting ensemble (LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost).

Version 1.0 DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23036631 Concept DOI (latest version): 10.5281/zenodo.23036630

Zindi Africa — Climate Risk and Health Prediction Challenge (AI4EAC)

Handle: @carnoba  |  Dates: 18 Aug 2026 – 19 Oct 2026  |  Prize Pool: $1,000 USD

Task: Geospatial prediction/classification of climate-sensitive child mortality risk in Uganda.

Result & Standing: Private Leaderboard Rank 384th / 1,626 participants (Top 23.6% overall, 960 active competitors) — achieved a Private Score of 0.8175 (within 0.0374 points of 1st place's 0.8549) across 10 targeted submissions.
